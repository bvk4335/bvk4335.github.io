Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

Pricing Strategy

A retail business had stable sales but declining profit margins, with no clear visibility into why. I analyzed 36 months of sales data across four product categories to find the root cause.

Approach
Exploratory data analysis and category-level break-even and price elasticity modeling using Python (Pandas, NumPy)
Finding
Over-discounting across ~60% of the product line was the primary driver of margin erosion — not weak sales or rising costs.
Recommended optimized discount thresholds with a modeled recovery of ₹59M in profitability.
Python · Pandas · NumPy · Statistical Analysis

BNPL Credit Risk — Data Product Analysis

Credit Risk

A Buy-Now-Pay-Later product needed to understand what actually drove customer defaults, across a global, varied customer base.

Approach
Correlation and segment analysis across 10,000+ transactions spanning 6 countries using Python
Finding
Credit score and risk score were the two strongest default predictors; app usage frequency and installment count had zero predictive power — despite being commonly assumed risk signals.
Identified a uniform installment policy gap and proposed a risk-tiered cap framework based on employment type and credit score.
Python · Correlation Analysis · Segmentation
